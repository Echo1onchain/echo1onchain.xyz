BullBears Giveaway Wheel - website version

Upload this whole folder (index.html AND the images folder together) to any website host.
The easiest free way:
  1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop (free; you'll make an account to keep the site).
  2. Drag this whole folder onto the page.
  3. Netlify gives you a web address. Open it and the giveaway wheel is there.
Other hosts work the same way (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, or your own site):
put index.html and the images folder side by side.

Good to know:
- Entries, winners and settings are saved in the browser you run it in.
  Use the same computer and browser each time; helpers can't add entries
  from their own devices on this version.
- Your settings, card background and 9 pointer badges are already built in.
- All four games are included: BullBear Race, Duck Race, Rocket Race and Rock Paper Scissors.
- Winner card background: in Winners, click Upload Image (or Change Image) and pick a
  PNG, JPG or WebP file. It's saved in your browser, so it stays when you come back.
  Use The Default switches back to the BullBears After Dark poster.
- To update the page later, upload the folder again.

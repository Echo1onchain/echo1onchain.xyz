Notes are mapped from the beats in each song. Long notes with a tail are holds: keep the key down while the line lights up, then let go before the next note for bonus points. The ECHO1 DJ bot dances harder at 10, 25 and 50 combos, scratches during holds, and glitches when you miss. Good hits fill the rainbow meters; when they’re full, Overdrive gives double points for 8 seconds. Click a key box to rebind it, tap the lanes on a phone, and press Esc to pause. The page music pauses while you play.

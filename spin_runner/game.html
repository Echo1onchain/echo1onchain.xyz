HOW TO DJ THE RUN

💿↔
STEERDrag the turntable left / right to slide your Bot between lanes.
⟳ ⟲
SPIN / REWINDSpin the platter clockwise to speed up (more points). Flick it counter-clockwise to REWIND: slow-mo + pass through anything. Cooldown ring shows when it's back.
🎚️⬆
JUMPPush the fader up. The higher you push, the higher you jump. It springs back on release.
♪ ⚡
THE DROPGrab notes to fill the DROP meter. When it flashes, WHIP the platter hard clockwise to boost and smash everything.
Phone: left thumb on the turntable, right thumb on the fader. Keyboard: ←/→ steer · Space hold = jump · ↑ spin · ↓ rewind · B drop
